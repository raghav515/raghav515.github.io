## Overview

The Unmanned Surface Vessel project turns an ordinary fishing canoe into an autonomous platform for search-and-rescue operations and maritime surveillance. I was part of a five-person team covering the vessel, its trailer, its steering and drive, and the simulation used to test its control.

## What I built

- A **modified fishing canoe with an onboard compute module** for autonomous navigation.
- A **custom canoe trailer designed in SolidWorks**, with half-ton capacity and an automated canoe-loading feature.
- A **ROS–Gazebo simulation environment** for testing control algorithms and autonomous navigation before touching the water.
- The **motor steering mechanism** and the control system enabling **RC operation**, on a canoe powered by both a petrol and an electric motor for optimized performance.

## Stack

ROS · Gazebo · SolidWorks · embedded control · petrol and electric propulsion
